Grönved Rymdcenter / space flight - third-party assets

All files are hosted locally with the game and are only downloaded by the
browser when the player approaches the spaceport. No third-party API is
contacted while playing. The original creators do not endorse this game.

Solar System Scope - Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
"Solar Textures" by Solar System Scope (INOVE), based on NASA elevation and
imagery data (incl. NASA Blue Marble).

Files: earth_day_8k.jpg, earth_night_8k.jpg, earth_clouds_8k.jpg,
earth_normal_4k.jpg, earth_specular_4k.jpg, moon_8k.jpg, mercury_4k.jpg,
venus_4k.jpg, mars_8k.jpg, jupiter_4k.jpg, saturn_4k.jpg, saturn_ring.png,
uranus_2k.jpg, neptune_2k.jpg, sun_2k.jpg, milky_way_8k.jpg
Modifications: TIFF normal/specular maps converted to JPEG and downscaled to
4K; some maps re-encoded to JPEG at a different quality; Mercury downscaled
to 4K. Shading (atmospheric scattering, night lights, ocean glint, relief,
ring shadows) is computed by the game at runtime.

ambientCG - CC0 1.0 Universal
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

Rock030: https://ambientcg.com/a/Rock030
Rock035: https://ambientcg.com/a/Rock035
Color, OpenGL normal, roughness and ambient-occlusion maps from the 2K JPG
packages, re-encoded. Used with triplanar projection on procedurally
generated asteroids.

The rocket "Nordstjärnan", launch complex LC-1, spacecraft, asteroid shapes,
exhaust, smoke, plasma and lens-flare effects are generated procedurally by
the game and are original designs.
